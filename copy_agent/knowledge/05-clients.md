## In-house build: the 8-Thon lead engine (real, shippable proof)

We built and run our own AI lead-generation system. Real, demonstrable
details for content:

- Discovery through data providers (Ocean.io) and Google Places, wired into
  one dashboard with background jobs and CSV export.
- A verified-email discipline: guessed, catch-all and placeholder addresses
  are filtered out in code. We found tracking-pixel and website-template
  junk (like literal filler@godaddy.com) polluting "verified" lists and
  built filters that kill it.
- Real run we can cite: one San Antonio construction search produced 31
  clean leads, 29 of them MX-verified, zero guessed addresses.
- Lesson we teach from it: a smaller verified list beats a big guessed one,
  and most "AI lead tools" quietly pad their numbers.
