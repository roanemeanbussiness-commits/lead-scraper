# YouTube Copy & Video Script Doctrine

Two jobs: **packaging** (title + thumbnail decide whether the video lives)
and **script copy** (the words that hold retention). Packaging first, always -
write the title and thumbnail text before the script.

## Packaging

**Titles** - provoke curiosity or promise extreme value without lying.
Under ~60 characters where possible. Draft 5-10 across these triggers, pick 3
finalists:
- Curiosity: "The Secret Feature Nobody Uses in [X]"
- Direct/search: "How to [Outcome] in 10 Minutes"
- Benefit: "Save 5 Hours a Week With This [X] Workflow"
- Stakes/negative: "Stop Doing [Common Practice] (It's Costing You Leads)"
- Numbered proof: "I Tested 7 AI Tools on a Real Business. One Won."

**Thumbnails** - readable on a phone at a glance: high contrast, one clear
subject, ≤3 words of text. Thumbnail and title must tell one micro-story
together without repeating each other's words.

## The Hook (0-30 seconds) - script it word-for-word

Structure: **cold open on the payoff or the problem** → stakes → credibility
flash → roadmap promise. No logo intros, no "hey guys welcome back", no
channel housekeeping. Deliver proof of the promise inside the first 30s.

Hook patterns:
- Result-first: "This system booked 27 sales calls last month. Here's the
  whole thing in 11 minutes."
- Threat: "If you're still [common practice], your competitors are quietly
  eating your pipeline. Let me show you what they're doing instead."
- Open loop: "By the end of this video you'll have [specific asset] - but
  first you need to see why the obvious way fails."

## Retention Structure

- `00:00` Hook - problem + promise immediately
- `00:45` Setup - minimal context + credibility
- `02:15` Core value #1
- `05:30` Pivot/stakes - the advanced move or the common mistake
- `08:45` Core value #2
- `11:20` Payoff - synthesize, show the final result
- `12:30` Hand-off - end screen CTA straight into the next video. Never end
  with "thanks for watching" dead air.

Rules: eliminate dead air and pacing drops; place payoffs just before
attention wanes; insert pattern interrupts (visual change, location change,
tempo change) roughly every 30-60 seconds; open loops early, close them late.

## Script Copy Rules

- Write for the ear: short sentences, contractions, second person.
- One idea per beat; a beat is 1-3 sentences then a visual change.
- Say the specific number, show the receipt on screen.
- Recap-and-reopen at each chapter boundary: close the last loop, open the
  next ("That fixes discovery. But none of it matters if the follow-up is
  slow - and that's where most businesses bleed.")
- CTAs mid-video are soft and earned ("the link's below if you want the
  template"); the end CTA routes to the next video, not five things at once.

## SEO & Metadata

- First 2 lines of description carry the search keywords naturally - they
  show in snippets.
- Chapters/timestamps for anything over ~4 minutes.
- 3 hashtags. Tags: primary keyword, variations, topic cluster.
- End screen links to the one video most likely to continue the session.

## Success Benchmarks

CTR 8%+ on new uploads · 50%+ retention at 3 minutes · 1%+ view-to-subscriber
conversion · first 24-hour performance above channel baseline. When metrics
are provided, diagnose: low CTR = packaging; early drop-off = hook; mid-video
drop = pacing/dead air; low session time = weak hand-off.

## Shorts / Repurposing

Every long-form video yields 2-4 shorts: the single sharpest claim, one
complete micro-how-to, one contrarian take. Shorts hook in the first line of
text on screen; captions always; loop the ending back to the opening line
when possible.
