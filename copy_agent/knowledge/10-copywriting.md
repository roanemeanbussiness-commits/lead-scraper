# Core Copywriting Doctrine

MindFluence (see skills/mindfluence.md) is the persuasion engine - bias
selection, anti-patterns, verification. This file is the baseline craft that
applies to every word regardless of bias stack.

## Core Principles

- **Clarity over cleverness.** If you must choose, choose clear.
- **Benefits over features.** Features: what it does. Benefits: what that
  means for the customer's Tuesday afternoon.
- **Specificity over vagueness.** "Cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15
  minutes" beats "save time on your workflow."
- **Customer language over company language.** Use the words the customer
  uses when complaining to a friend.
- **One idea per section.** Each section advances one argument.

## Proven Hook Formulas

1. **Observation + Stat + Contrast + Promise** - "I've been watching [trend].
   [Specific stat]. Most people think [X]; what's true is [Y]. Here's what to do."
2. **Personal Limitation + Achievement** - "I can't [thing]. But this week I
   [impressive result despite it]."
3. **Social Proof Opening** - "I [talked to / worked with] [specific number]
   [impressive group]. [Surprising finding]."
4. **Contrarian Challenge** - "Everyone says [common wisdom]. That's
   surface-level. What actually matters is [insight]."
5. **Simple Declarative** - "[Bold, clear statement that frames the piece]."
6. **Harsh Reality** - "Harsh reality: [uncomfortable truth most avoid]."

Subheadline patterns: "[Achieve outcome] without [pain point]" · "Turn
[input] into [outcome]" · "[Number] [people] use [thing] to [outcome]".

## Authority Through Specificity

Vague claims destroy credibility; specific numbers create it. Revenue
thresholds ("$13M+"), exact stats ("3.2x", "95%"), time frames ("in 48
hours"), group sizes ("40 founders"). If a specific number doesn't exist,
flag it - never substitute vague language, never invent one.

## CTA Guidelines

Weak (avoid): Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here, Get Started.
Strong: action verb + what they get ("Get My Free Audit"), outcome-focused
("Start Saving 10 Hours a Week"), specific ("Book a 30-Minute Strategy
Call"), first-person ("Start My Free Trial").

## Banned Words

streamline, optimize, innovative, game-changing, cutting-edge, robust,
seamless, leverage, synergize, holistic, best-in-class, world-class,
disruptive, revolutionary. Replace with the specific outcome.

## Page Structure (standard order)

Hero → social proof bar → problem → solution/how it works (3-4 steps) → key
benefits (3-5 with proof) → testimonials (real names, specific results) →
FAQ/objections → final CTA with risk reversal.

## Style Rules

Short paragraphs (max 3 sentences). Active voice. No exclamation points.
Bold sparingly. Read it out loud - if it sounds unnatural, rewrite.

## Output Habits

For any substantial piece, provide: copy organized by section, 3 headline
options with rationale, 2-3 CTA options, and brief annotations explaining
key choices.
